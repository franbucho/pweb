What I do

Connecting technology
with the way people work.

I’m an RPA and AI automation developer based in New York, originally from Venezuela. I spent more than six years building automation for enterprise teams: 50+ production UiPath and Power Platform automations at a Big Four firm, global RPA quality standards across regional hubs, and contract and financial-data bots for an insurance broker operating in three countries.

Today I work independently, combining classic RPA with AI agents. I run my own 24/7 automation server, where Claude-based agents with MCP connectors handle email triage, reports, monitoring and even code review, all controlled from my phone. I bring the same discipline to client work: understand the process first, automate what is stable, log everything, keep secrets in a vault and plan for exceptions.

I work in English and Spanish, remotely or on-site in the New York area.

For me, a successful solution should be easy to understand, well documented, and able to evolve as a business grows. I care about what happens after a project is delivered: how people use it, how it handles exceptions, and whether it continues to solve the right problem.
