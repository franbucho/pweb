01

24/7 AI Agent Operations Server

Problem: Routine digital chores (inbox checks, reports, monitoring, follow-ups) ate hours every week and needed someone at a computer.

Solution: A Linux server running Claude Code around the clock, controlled by chat from a phone. A coordinator agent hands work to specialized agents (developer, QA, documentation, monitoring) that run on cron schedules and report back on Telegram. The system monitors and repairs itself, and secrets live in a password vault.

Result: Runs 24/7 unattended, accepts voice-note commands, and keeps a live status board of every agent.

02

Nightly Report Pipeline

Problem: Every night a hotel operation received a dozen system-generated reports by email that had to be downloaded, renamed, filed and summarized by hand.

Solution: An agent that collects the 12 reports from the inbox, names and files them in Google Drive, and prepares the consolidated report email with the key KPIs (occupancy, ADR, revenue) for a human to review.

Result: The nightly filing and summary step went from manual to automated.

ClaudeMCP (Gmail, Google Drive)PythonPrivate
03

Delivery-Platform Reconciliation Bots

Problem: A restaurant group had to log into several delivery platforms and its POS every week to download payout reports and reconcile them by hand.

Solution: Modular Selenium bots, one per platform, behind a small desktop app where staff choose the platforms and date range. The bots download the payout reports, file them and produce a reconciliation CSV, logging each run’s status to Google Sheets and saving a screenshot when something fails.

Result: Weekly reconciliation became a one-click run with an audit trail.

PythonSeleniumpandasTkintergspread (Google Sheets)Private
04

Threat Analyzer Bot

Problem: Staff receive suspicious attachments on their phones and open them before anyone can check them.

Solution: A messaging bot and web service. Users forward a file, it is scanned against dozens of antivirus engines through the VirusTotal API, and the user gets a clear “safe to open / don’t open” answer.

Result: Working prototype for mobile-device threat triage.

PythonFlaskVirusTotal APIWhatsApp bot integrationPrivate
05

Niria: AI Eye-Health Pre-screening

AI eye-health pre-screening web app — product & development.

Problem: People put off eye check-ups and don’t know when a symptom needs a specialist.

Solution: A web app that combines an eye photo with a short health questionnaire, runs an AI analysis and generates a PDF report with nearby specialists. It includes role-based portals for patients, doctors and HR teams.

Result: Full product prototype with payments, three languages (EN/ES/ZH) and an admin dashboard.

ReactTypeScriptFirebase (Auth, Firestore, Functions)Google Gemini APIStripeTailwindPrivate
06

RPA Analyzer

Problem: Before quoting an automation, you need to know how hard a web page will be to automate.

Solution: A Python tool that loads any URL headless and counts the interactive elements (buttons, inputs, dropdowns), detects the platform and HTTPS, and gives a quick complexity read.

Result: Speeds up automation scoping and estimates. Open source.
